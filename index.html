Services

What we do

We take on the roles that make international partnerships work: finding the opportunity, building the team, managing delivery and making the results last.

1

EU funding & consortium building

Identifying suitable calls, matching partners across Europe and Africa, and preparing competitive proposals.

2

Project management & coordination

Planning, budgets, partner coordination, risk management and reporting to funders, from kick-off to final report.

3

Stakeholder engagement in Africa

Working with ministries, public agencies, local authorities, NGOs, universities and the private sector to secure local ownership.

4

Communication & dissemination

Communication plans, events, publications and multilingual outreach that put project results in front of the people who use them.

5

Monitoring, evaluation & impact

Indicators, data collection, evaluation and sustainability plans that show what changed and keep it going after funding ends.

6

Europe–Africa business connections

Market entry support, partner search and introductions for companies and investors working between the two continents.

About

An EU company built for Europe–Africa cooperation

Tsolepou International was founded in Tenerife in 2020 as a management consultancy dedicated to project management and business connections between Europe and Africa. We work alongside universities, public institutions, NGOs and companies on both continents, bringing the coordination and local engagement that international projects need.

Affiliated organisation: Tsolepou Foundation

Tsolepou Foundation is an independent non-profit foundation linked to our founder. Its social-impact work in Africa shares our commitment to cooperation between the two continents.
