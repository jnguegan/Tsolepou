1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, do comparable things.

2. Cookies used on this site

This website uses no analytics, advertising, social media or tracking cookies. It also contains no third-party content that would set them (videos, maps or social buttons).

We only store one technical value in your browser's local storage to remember that you closed the cookie notice, so it is not shown again:

Namets_cookie_notice
TypeLocal storage, technical, first-party
PurposeRemember that you closed the notice
DurationUntil you clear it in your browser

Under article 22.2 of Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE), strictly necessary storage requested by the user does not require consent.

3. Managing or deleting cookies

You can clear or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge and others).

4. Changes

If we add non-essential cookies in future (for example analytics), we will update this policy and ask for your prior consent through a banner with options to accept or reject them.
